ABOUT FDK

Better Trading Starts With Understanding Yourself.

FDK is an independent trading education project focused on the decisions, habits, emotions, and behaviors that influence how we trade.

THE PURPOSE

More Than Knowing What to Do

Trading education often focuses on strategies, chart patterns, and finding opportunities. But knowing what to do does not always mean we will do it when the pressure is on.

Fear can make us close a winning trade too early. Frustration can push us into another trade before we have thought it through. A losing streak can make us question a strategy that we have not properly evaluated.

FDK explores these situations and encourages traders to examine not only what happened in a trade, but also why they made the decisions they did.

THE PERSON BEHIND FDK

A Learning Journey, Shared Honestly

I'm Walter. I created FDK to share lessons, observations, and experiences from my journey in trading.

I'm not presenting myself as someone who has mastered trading or figured everything out. I'm interested in understanding the behaviors that affect our decisions, learning from mistakes, and finding practical ways to improve.

Some of the ideas shared here come from my own experiences and observations. Others are general educational principles that traders can explore and evaluate for themselves.

My aim is not to tell you that I have all the answers. It's to share useful perspectives that might help you ask better questions about your own trading.

"You can understand a mistake and still find yourself repeating it. Improvement begins when you start recognizing the behavior, especially when it matters most."

— Walter, FDK

OUR APPROACH

Understand. Reflect. Improve.

01

Understand

Learn about common trading behaviors, emotional triggers, risk management, and the mistakes that can affect decision-making.

02

Reflect

Examine your own decisions honestly. Ask what happened, what influenced your actions, and whether you followed your plan.

03

Improve

Use what you learn to identify specific behaviors to repeat, adjust, or stop, then review whether your actions actually change.

YOUR PART IN THE PROCESS

Guidance Is Only the Beginning

Reading about discipline does not automatically make you disciplined. Understanding risk does not guarantee that you will manage it well when emotions are involved.

The real work happens when you apply what you learn, observe your own behavior, review your decisions, and make an effort to improve over time.

You will not get everything right every time. The goal is to become more aware of what you do, understand why you do it, and take responsibility for what you choose to change.
