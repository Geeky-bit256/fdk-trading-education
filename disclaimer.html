IMPORTANT INFORMATION

Trading Education & Disclaimer

FDK shares educational material to encourage reflection, awareness, and more thoughtful trading decisions. It is important to understand the limits of this information before applying it.

1. Educational Purposes Only

The content on FDK is provided for general educational and informational purposes. It is intended to help readers understand trading concepts, risk management, trading psychology, journaling, and decision-making.

The content is not personalized financial, investment, or trading advice. It does not take your individual financial circumstances, objectives, experience, or risk tolerance into account.

2. Trading Involves Risk

Trading financial markets involves risk, and you may lose some or all of the money you commit. Losses can occur even when you follow a strategy or make a decision that was reasonable based on the information available at the time.

Risk management can help you plan and control exposure, but it cannot eliminate risk or guarantee that your account will be protected from losses.

Never assume that a strategy, technique, or idea discussed on FDK is suitable for your circumstances. Consider your financial position and the risks involved before making trading decisions.

3. Personal Experiences and Examples

Some content may include personal experiences, observations, hypothetical scenarios, or examples intended to explain a concept.

Personal experiences are not proof that the same outcome will occur for someone else. Examples are provided for learning and discussion, not as promises of future results.

Past performance does not guarantee future results. Individual outcomes can differ depending on many factors, including market conditions, execution, costs, strategy, and risk management.

4. No Guarantee of Results

Reading FDK, applying its lessons, or keeping a trading journal does not guarantee profitability, consistent performance, or the elimination of trading mistakes.

Educational material can support your learning, but improvement requires your own effort, practice, evaluation, and judgment. You remain responsible for deciding how to use the information provided.

5. Your Responsibility

You are responsible for your own trading decisions, risk management, and use of the information on FDK. You should evaluate ideas critically rather than treating them as instructions to enter or exit a trade.

Where appropriate, consider seeking guidance from a suitably qualified financial professional who understands your circumstances.

6. Independent Educational Project

FDK is an independent educational project. Unless explicitly stated otherwise, its content should not be interpreted as representing a brokerage, financial institution, or other third party.

References to markets, platforms, instruments, companies, or services are for educational context and do not necessarily imply endorsement or affiliation.

A FINAL REMINDER

Learning Is Only the Beginning

FDK can provide ideas, questions, and guidance to help you reflect on your trading. The effort of applying what you learn, reviewing your own decisions, and developing your judgment remains yours.

Take what is useful, examine it critically, and remember that no educational resource can remove the uncertainty involved in trading.
